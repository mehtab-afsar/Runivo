Every run claims real ground. Run a street to make it yours, close a loop to own everything inside it, and take zones from rivals in your city. Runivo is a GPS running tracker, a territory game and a free AI coach in one app.

CLAIM YOUR CITY
• Run a street and it becomes your zone, drawn from your GPS route
• Close a loop around a park or a block to claim the whole area inside
• Run through a rival's zone to take part of it
• Zones fade if you leave them, so keep running them to hold your ground
• See your zones and nearby rivals on a live map

PACEX, YOUR AI RUNNING COACH
• A debrief after every run: what went well, what to work on
• A weekly review of your running and your plan
• Ask anything about pace, recovery or race prep
• Every Monday, PaceX checks last week and offers to ease your plan if you missed sessions. Nothing changes without your OK

TRAINING PLANS
• Plans for 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon
• Built around the days you can run and your race date
• Each run you record ticks off its session automatically
• A reminder the evening before each session

TRACK EVERY RUN
• GPS route, distance, pace, splits and elevation
• Keeps tracking with your screen locked
• Live stats on your lock screen while you run
• Optional auto-pause and voice updates
• Save runs to Apple Health if you allow it; your past workouts set your starting fitness

RUN WITH FRIENDS
• See which friends are running right now and cheer them on mid-run
• Share runs, give kudos, comment and follow other runners
• Start or join a club, chat with clubmates and join events
• Weekly, monthly and all-time leaderboards
• Share cards for every run, zone and year

REWARDS AND MISSIONS
• Daily missions and streaks to keep you moving
• Earn PACE points by running. PACE is free to earn and can't be bought
• Spend PACE on rewards in the Store where partners are available

PRIVACY AND SAFETY
• Keep home private: hide your zones and set private places
• Profile visibility: everyone, followers you approve, or only you
• Live location sharing is off until you turn it on
• Send a live safety link to someone you trust during a run
• Report or block anyone, any time

COMPLETELY FREE
Tracking, territory, PaceX, training plans, clubs, the feed and missions are all free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases.

PaceX and Foot scan send your run and fitness data, or the photo you take, to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic), only after you give permission. PaceX gives general running guidance, not medical advice.
