Runivo turns running into a game played on the real map. Run a street or a loop to claim it as your territory, defend it, and take ground back from other runners in your city.

HOW IT WORKS
Every run you record can claim a zone: a strip along the route you ran, or the whole area inside a loop you close. Run through a rival's zone to take part of it. Zones fade if you leave them alone, so keep running them.

TERRITORY MAP
See your zones and nearby rivals on a live map. Zones are real shapes from your GPS route, sized by how far you ran.

RUN TRACKING
GPS route, pace, splits and elevation, with tracking that continues while your screen is locked. Optional auto-pause. Completed runs can be saved to Apple Health if you allow it.

PACE REWARDS AND MISSIONS
Earn PACE by running and completing daily missions. PACE is free to earn and cannot be bought. In selected cities, PACE unlocks partner offers in the Store.

CLUBS AND EVENTS
Join or start a club, chat with clubmates, and take part in club and community events.

FEED AND LEADERBOARDS
Share your runs, follow other runners, like and comment. Weekly, monthly and all-time leaderboards by territory, distance and PACE.

GEAR LOG
Track mileage on each pair of running shoes.

PRIVACY AND SAFETY
Keep your home out of the game with private places and hide-my-zones. Live location sharing is off by default. Share a live safety link with someone you trust during a run. Report or block anyone, any time.

FREE AND RUNIVO PLUS
Tracking, territory, clubs, the feed, missions and the Store are free. Free accounts can hold up to 10 zones at a time.

Runivo Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription that adds unlimited zones, the PaceX AI running coach (post-run debriefs, weekly briefs and chat), training plans, hosting events, extra map styles and a higher weekly PACE cap. The price and billing period are shown in the app before you subscribe. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple ID account settings.

PaceX sends your run and fitness data to a third-party AI provider, only after you give permission.
